RGVM8 Investing Starter Guide

Building wealth isn't about picking the next big stock. It's about consistency, patience, and not getting in your own way. Here's where to start:

1. Build your safety net first
Save 3-6 months of expenses in a high-yield savings account before investing a dollar. This keeps a bad month from turning into a bad year.

2. Take the free money
If your employer matches 401(k) contributions, contribute enough to get the full match first. It's an instant, guaranteed return nothing else can match.

3. Keep it simple and diversified
Low-cost, broad index funds (S&P 500 or total market) beat most people trying to pick individual stocks, with a fraction of the stress.

4. Invest on a schedule, not a feeling
Dollar-cost averaging -- investing the same amount on the same schedule, regardless of what the market's doing -- beats trying to time the highs and lows.

5. Kill high-interest debt first
Credit card debt at 20%+ interest will outrun almost any investment return. Pay that off before investing aggressively.

6. Watch lifestyle creep
When your income goes up, let your investments go up with it -- not just your spending. That gap is what actually builds wealth over time.

This is general education, not personalized financial advice. Talk to a licensed financial advisor about your specific situation before making investment decisions.
